About our signals and performance statistics

  • Signals come from an automated trading system and show direction, entry, stop loss and take profit.
  • Outcomes (“TP hit”, “SL hit”, “Expired”) are worked out automatically by comparing each signal with historical price data from a third-party provider. If both levels are reached within the same one-minute candle, we record a stop-loss hit.
  • Statistics assume the trade is opened at the published entry price at the time of publication. They do not include spreads, commissions, swaps, slippage, delays or rejected orders. Your broker’s prices differ from our data source, so your real results can be better or worse.
  • Results are shown in R multiples (a loss is −1R, a win is the reward-to-risk ratio of that signal), not in money.
  • Past performance is not a reliable guide to future results. A good run can end at any time.
