3. Cookies and similar technologies

See our Cookie Policy for the full list. You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the page footer.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We use service providers (processors) who handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database for articles, newsletter subscribers and signals.
  • Netlify: website hosting and delivery.
  • Google: Tag Manager, Analytics, AdSense and Fonts.
  • TradingView: embedded price ticker, market and calendar widgets (they may set their own cookies).
  • Content delivery and image hosts such as jsDelivr, cdnjs, Unsplash and DiceBear, plus Open-Meteo for weather. These receive your IP address when your browser loads their files.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Our providers operate in several countries, including outside Uganda, the EEA and the UK. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we rely on the mechanisms our providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent protections.

6. How long we keep data

  • Newsletter emails: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Published articles: until you or we remove them.
  • Analytics: according to the retention period set in our analytics account.
  • Emails and messages: as long as needed to deal with your request, then deleted or archived.

7. Your rights

Uganda (Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019): you may ask to access your personal data, have it corrected or deleted, withdraw consent, and object to processing, including processing for direct marketing. You may complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO), hosted by NITA-U.

EEA / UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): you may ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You may complain to your national data-protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office).

To use any of these rights, email nwmnybdhn@gmail.com. We may need to verify who you are and will normally reply within 30 days.

8. Children

The site is not directed at anyone under 18 and trading content is unsuitable for minors. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our database. No online service is completely secure, so please do not share sensitive information with us.

10. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
