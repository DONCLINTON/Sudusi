How results are tracked

Method

When a signal is published we assume it is entered at the published entry price. A server job checks 1-minute price candles after that time. For a BUY, the first candle whose low reaches the stop loss is a loss, and the first whose high reaches the take profit is a win. For a SELL it is the other way round. If both levels fall inside the same candle we count it as a stop-loss hit. Signals that hit neither level within 48 hours are marked Expired and are not counted in the win rate.

Limits

Prices come from a third-party data feed and differ from your broker. The method ignores spread, commission, swap and slippage. Real results will differ. Past results do not predict future results.
